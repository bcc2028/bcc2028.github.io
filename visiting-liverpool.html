Visiting Liverpool

Conference venue

University of Liverpool, Liverpool, UK

The University’s main campus is in the city centre. The conference buildings, rooms and step-free routes to them will be listed here once the venues are confirmed.

University campus maps

Getting here

From Liverpool Lime Street, the main campus is about ten minutes away on foot. From the station’s main entrance, head towards the Adelphi Hotel, then follow Brownlow Hill uphill towards the University.
